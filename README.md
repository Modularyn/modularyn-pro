# Modularyn Pro

This repository will hold the source of Modularyn's paid modules: the modules licensed by the Standard and Pro editions. The source is published so that anyone can review it. It is not open source.

## What the paid modules are

Modularyn's Community edition is free under the Apache License 2.0, commercial use included, and is complete without the paid editions; it lives in [Modularyn/modularyn](https://github.com/Modularyn/modularyn). Standard and Pro license the same set of paid modules, Standard for one person and Pro for teams and organizations:

- the SaaS commercial lifecycle;
- Studio, the developer workspace;
- governed low-code and AI-assisted authoring;
- AI management;
- workflow.

The paid modules depend on Community only through published Community packages pinned to exact versions, and Community never depends on them. The [edition map](https://github.com/Modularyn/modularyn/blob/main/docs/editions/README.md) assigns every catalog entry to its edition.

## License

The source in this repository is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md). Noncommercial use, as that license defines it, is permitted. Commercial use requires a separate license, and none is offered yet; there are no offers or prices.

Five rules apply to every paid service:

1. Every paid service has a self-hosted path at the edition that sells it.
2. No access to your applications or data requires a Modularyn account.
3. Moving between hosted and self-hosted loses nothing, in either direction.
4. Paid rights are verified offline and outlive Modularyn's servers.
5. Ending a subscription ends only the service, never self-hosting of covered releases or any Community right.

## Status

Modularyn is in planning, and nothing has been released. No paid module exists yet; they are planned for later delivery gates, after the Community foundation. The [roadmap](https://github.com/Modularyn/modularyn/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) lists the milestones in order, as plans rather than promises.
